# Regulatory Classification & Legal Opinion Brief
## World Money: Non-Custodial Financial Data & Intelligence Provider

---

## 1. Operating Model Summary

The World Money (Antigravity) terminal operates strictly as a **Financial Intelligence Software & Market Data Provider** (equivalent to Bloomberg B-PIPE or Refinitiv Eikon):

1. **Non-Custodial Architecture**:
   - The platform never holds, transmits, clears, or custodies customer or institutional funds.
   - All order simulation endpoints execute in local paper/dry-run sandbox mode or connect directly to customer-owned third-party broker API keys stored in client-side AES-256 local vaults.
2. **FinCEN MSB Exemption**:
   - Under FinCEN Guidance FIN-2019-G001, providers of pure software, communications protocols, and market analytics without independent transmission or custody authority are **exempt from Money Services Business (MSB) registration**.
3. **Data Protection & Privacy**:
   - **GDPR & UK DPA**: Client analytics support client-side masked PII (Analyst Role) and 90-day data retention schedules.
   - **RBI Data Localization (India)**: Local sovereign note generation and zero off-shore transaction transmission.

## 2. Customer Pilot Contracting Framework

For institutional 2-week pilots (e.g. JPMorgan, Bank of America, Wise):
- **Contract Form**: Enterprise Data Processing Agreement (DPA) + Standard Software Evaluation Agreement (SEA).
- **Compliance Footprint**: Zero funds transmission licensing required for pilot initiation.
