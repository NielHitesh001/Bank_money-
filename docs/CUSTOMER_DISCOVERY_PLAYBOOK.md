## 3. Discovery Call Questionnaire (30-Min Framework)

1. **"Walk me through your current process for monitoring corridor settlement health across emerging markets."**
2. **"What is the operational or financial impact when an emerging market payment rail experiences unannounced clearing delays?"**
3. **"If your desk had programmatic sub-5-second visibility into SWIFT + local rail health, how would your daily routing or liquidity positioning change?"**
4. **"Who on your team evaluates new treasury visibility software, and what is your typical procurement timeline?"**
