# World Money — Customer Discovery Operations Playbook
## 4-Week Execution Framework for Institutional & FinTech Pilots

---

## 🎯 Dual-Track Outreach Strategy

### Track A: FinTech Fast-Track (Cold / Direct Outreach)
- **Target Audience**: Wise, Remitly, Payoneer, Revolut, Checkout.com.
- **Decision Makers**: Head of Settlement, VP of Platform Engineering, Head of Treasury Operations.
- **Sales Velocity**: 1–2 week decision cycle, standard cloud procurement.
- **Value Prop**: Eliminates $500K+/year in settlement delays with sub-second API rail status feeds.

### Track B: Institutional Banks (Warm / Advisor-Led)
- **Target Audience**: JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citi, HSBC, Goldman Sachs.
- **Decision Makers**: Head of Global Liquidity, SVP Treasury Operations.
- **Sales Velocity**: Requires executive sponsor or mutual advisor intro; 4–8 week pilot initiation.
- **Value Prop**: Replaces 4h/day manual corridor tracking across 100+ emerging market clearing systems.

---

## 📞 Discovery Call Script & Qualification Matrix

### 1. Opening (2 Minutes)
> "Hi [Name]. I'm Neel, founder of World Money. We've built a real-time liquidity visibility platform that tracks SWIFT status, local payment rails, and central bank policy rates with sub-5-second updates. 
> Before showing anything, could you walk me through your current process for monitoring corridor settlement health across emerging markets?"

### 2. Pain & Incident Cost (8 Minutes)
- **Question 1**: *"How does your ops desk currently detect when a local clearing rail (e.g. PIX in Brazil, UPI in India, or local RTGS) is experiencing maintenance or degraded settlement?"*
- **Question 2**: *"What is the financial or customer impact when an unexpected clearing delay occurs on a high-volume corridor?"* (Listen for: \$50K+ incident costs, manual reconciliations).

### 3. Buying & Pilot Intent (5 Minutes)
- **Question 3**: *"If your desk had programmatic sub-second visibility into SWIFT + local rail health with an immutable compliance audit trail, would that solve this bottleneck?"*
- **Question 4 (The Close)**: *"Would you be open to a 2-week zero-risk pilot on your top 3 corridors where we provide dedicated dashboard access and measure routing accuracy and time-to-discovery?"*

---

## 🚦 Decision Flags
- 🟢 **Green Flags**: Prospect shares a specific recent \$50K+ delay incident, requests API integration specs, introduces compliance/CRO.
- 🔴 **Red Flags**: "We're happy with manual Bloomberg checks", "Check back in Q2 next year", unwillingness to name high-volume corridors.
