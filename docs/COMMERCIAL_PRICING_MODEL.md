# World Money Commercial Pricing & Revenue Model

## 1. Production Subscription Tiers

### Tier 1: Small Liquidity Desk
- **Price**: **\$5,000 / month** (\$60,000 ARR)
- **Scope**: Up to 25 corridors, 5 authenticated users.
- **SLA**: 99.5% uptime, 1-hour priority support.
- **Includes**: REST API, Webhook notifications, daily audit exports.

### Tier 2: Mid-Market Liquidity Operations
- **Price**: **\$15,000 / month** (\$180,000 ARR)
- **Scope**: Up to 100 corridors, 15 authenticated users.
- **SLA**: 99.9% uptime, 30-min SLA response.
- **Includes**: Real-time WebSocket streaming, custom clearing integrations, dedicated account manager.

### Tier 3: Global Enterprise / Tier-1 Bank
- **Price**: **\$25,000 – \$50,000+ / month** (\$300,000 – \$600,000+ ARR)
- **Scope**: Unlimited corridors (all global & EM rails), unlimited users, multi-region dedicated infrastructure.
- **SLA**: 99.99% high-availability SLA, 24/7 dedicated engineering support.
- **Includes**: Private VPC deployment, SOC 2 Type II compliance attestation, bespoke quantitative ML regime feeds.
