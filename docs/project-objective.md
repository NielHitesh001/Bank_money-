
## **Executive & Pitch Deck**

- "Developing a unified, single-screen global financial dashboard mapping the end-to-end flow of capital—from international institutions like the World Bank and private powerhouses like JPMorgan down to the individual consumer."
    
## **Product Vision & Technical**

- "A comprehensive global liquidity map designed to visualize macro-to-micro capital flows across the entire financial ecosystem on a single interactive interface."
    
## **Elevator Pitch**

- "We are building a single-screen global map that traces money from major public and private financial institutions all the way down to individual transactions."
    

## **Investor Summary**

- "Visualizing the complete global financial pipeline: an interactive platform mapping capital distribution from supranational entities and commercial megabanks directly to end users."
    
## **Key Terms to Feature**

- **Macro-to-micro financial flows:** Highlights tracking from institutions down to individuals.
    
- **Unified capital tracking / Global liquidity map:** Refines "World trading map."
    
- **End-to-end financial pipeline:** Describes the complete path from banks to people.
